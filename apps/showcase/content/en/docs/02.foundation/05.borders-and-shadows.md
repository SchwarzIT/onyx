Borders and shadows define boundaries, separate content areas, and convey elevation and hierarchy. Borders provide crisp structure, while shadows add subtle depth cues to interactive and floating elements.

## Borders

Borders delineate containers, define component edges, and indicate interactive states such as focus, hover, or validation.

To ensure consistent rounding across interfaces, we provide semantic border-radius variables. These range from subtle corners to fully rounded pills and circular shapes.

:border-radius-variables

## Shadows

Box shadows add elevation to floating containers such as flyouts, popovers, modals, and toasts, separating them from underlying content.

::info-card{headline="Shadows in dark mode" color="warning"}
Shadows provide poor contrast on dark backgrounds. Consequently, all shadow variables resolve to `none` in dark mode. For dark mode elevation, use a border (such as `--onyx-color-component-border-neutral`) or background color differentiation.
::

:shadow-variables
