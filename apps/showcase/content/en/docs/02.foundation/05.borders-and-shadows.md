Borders and shadows are essential foundational styling elements that define boundaries, separate content areas, and convey elevation and hierarchy. While borders provide crisp contours and structure, shadows add subtle depth and elevation cues to interactive and floating elements.

## Borders

Borders help delineate containers, define component edges, and indicate interactive states (such as focus, hover, or validation).

To ensure consistent rounding of shapes and elements across all interfaces, we provide semantic border-radius variables. These range from subtle rounding for small elements and controls to fully rounded pills and circular shapes. Using our radius variables ensures visual harmony and coherent styling across applications.

:border-radius-variables

## Shadows

Box shadows add visual depth and elevation to elements, helping floating containers such as flyouts, popovers, modals, and toasts stand out from background layers.

::info-card{headline="Shadows in dark mode" color="warning"}
Shadows do not work effectively in dark mode because dark backgrounds naturally absorb shadows, making elevation invisible. Therefore, all our shadow variables evaluate to `none` (no shadow) in dark mode. When designing components or custom layouts, use a border (e.g. `--onyx-color-component-border-neutral`) or subtle background color differentiation in dark mode to clearly define boundaries and convey elevation instead.
::
