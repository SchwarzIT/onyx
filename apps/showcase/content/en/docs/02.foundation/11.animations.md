## Principles

Animations should clarify interactions rather than distract. Use motion purposefully to indicate state changes, provide feedback, and maintain orientation.

## Accessibility

Excessive or unexpected motion can trigger symptoms in users with [vestibular motion disorders](https://www.a11yproject.com/posts/understanding-vestibular-disorders/).
Respect the `prefers-reduced-motion` media query by disabling non-essential transitions when requested by the user's system preferences.
