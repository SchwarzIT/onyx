## Principles

Images convey messages quickly, establishing mood and supporting understanding. Follow these principles when incorporating photography:

<steps>

::step
#headline
Color gamut

#default
Select images where a single primary hue dominates the composition.

<layout-container>
<image-do-dont-card src="/images/color-gamut-pro.webp" alt="Green container" color="success">
Choose images with a harmonious, monochromatic, or analogous color profile.
</image-do-dont-card>

<image-do-dont-card src="/images/color-gamut-con.webp" alt="Multiple containers with different mixed colors" color="danger">
Avoid images with competing, oversaturated primary colors.
</image-do-dont-card>
</layout-container>

**Tip:** Vary color gamuts across sections to provide subtle navigation cues.
::

::step
#headline
Natural lighting

#default
Prioritize soft, authentic light sources to maintain a realistic aesthetic.

<layout-container>
<image-do-dont-card src="/images/natural-light-pro.webp" alt="Two men sitting at a table looking at each other with natural sun light" color="success">
Ensure lighting is naturally balanced and subjects appear evenly lit.
</image-do-dont-card>

<image-do-dont-card src="/images/natural-light-con.webp" alt="Man sitting in a dark room in front of a computer" color="danger">
Avoid harsh highlights, deep shadows, or high-contrast studio flash.
</image-do-dont-card>
</layout-container>

**Tip:** Avoid heavy filters or over-processing that make photos look artificial.
::

::step
#headline
Authentic snapshots

#default
Choose candid imagery that feels unscripted rather than staged.

<layout-container>
<image-do-dont-card src="/images/snapshot-pro.webp" alt="Group of people in a creative meeting room looking at a whiteboard with sticky notes" color="success">
Look for natural movement and genuine interaction.
</image-do-dont-card>

<image-do-dont-card src="/images/snapshot-con.webp" alt="Group of people sitting at a desk looking at paper sheets with unnatural conversation" color="danger">
Avoid forced expressions, unnatural poses, or generic stock-photo smiles.
</image-do-dont-card>
</layout-container>

**Tip:** Photos should resemble authentic moments in real working environments.
::

::step
#headline
Visual simplicity

#default
Select minimalist compositions that emphasize a single subject or message.

<layout-container>
<image-do-dont-card src="/images/visual-simplicity-pro.webp" alt="Hand holding a credit card" color="success">
Choose images with generous negative space and uncluttered backgrounds.
</image-do-dont-card>

<image-do-dont-card src="/images/visual-simplicity-con.webp" alt="Hand holding a wallet with a a bunch of credit cards" color="danger">
Avoid cluttered scenes with competing focal points.
</image-do-dont-card>
</layout-container>

**Tip:** Simple framing reduces visual noise and keeps content clear.
::

::step
#headline
Direct authenticity

#default
Use genuine photography without artificial digital embellishments.

<layout-container>
<image-do-dont-card src="/images/image-authenticity-pro.webp" alt="Authentic group of young people talking to each other" color="success">
Use naturally composed photography.
</image-do-dont-card>

<image-do-dont-card src="/images/image-authenticity-con.webp" alt="Cross of four arms with an artificial 'Teamwork' text placed over the image" color="danger">
Do not overlay artificial slogans, 3D elements, or synthetic graphics on photos.
</image-do-dont-card>
</layout-container>

**Tip:** Imagery should look captured by a camera, not rendered artificially.
::

::step
#headline
Technical quality

#default
Ensure assets meet resolution and compression standards. Optimize image dimensions for their display container to avoid serving unnecessary 4K assets on small viewports.

Use modern formats like [WebP](https://developers.google.com/speed/webp/docs/cwebp) to minimize file sizes.

<layout-container>
<image-do-dont-card src="/images/image-quality-pro.webp" alt="High resolution container ship" color="success">
Use sharp, properly scaled assets.
</image-do-dont-card>

<image-do-dont-card src="/images/image-quality-con.webp" alt="Very low resolution container ship" color="danger">
Avoid assets with visible compression artifacts, noise, or motion blur.
</image-do-dont-card>
</layout-container>

**Tip:** If pixelation or compression artifacts are noticeable, replace the asset.
::

</steps>

## Accessibility

Every informative image must provide descriptive alternative text (`alt`). Screen readers rely on alt text to convey meaning to visually impaired users, and it also serves as fallback content if image loading fails. Decorative images should use an empty `alt=""` attribute.

## Technical usage

Use the [OnyxImage](/components/basic/image) component to render images. It enforces accessible attributes and provides built-in lazy-loading and skeleton states.
