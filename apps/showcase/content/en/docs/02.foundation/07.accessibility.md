## Our commitment

To ensure products are usable by everyone, we are **committed to WCAG 2.2 Level AA compliance** across all components.

The [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), developed by the Web Accessibility Initiative (WAI), provide the benchmark framework:

<steps>

::step
#headline
Level A

#default
Addresses fundamental barriers—such as basic keyboard navigation—required for digital usability.
::

::step
#headline
Level AA <onyx-tag label="target standard" color="success" />

#default
Establishes strong contrast, readability, and predictable interaction, satisfying legal accessibility standards.
::

::step
#headline
Level AAA

#default
The strictest compliance tier, featuring advanced requirements such as dedicated sign language interpretation.
::

</steps>

## Key principles

Our components adhere to three core accessibility standards:

<steps>

::step
#headline
ARIA patterns

#default
Components implement standard [W3C WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/) to deliver familiar, predictable interaction patterns for assistive technologies.
::

::step
#headline
Assistive technology support

#default
Components feature full keyboard operability and structured screen reader announcements. Accessible labels and descriptions are required by default.
::

::step
#headline
Color contrast

#default
Palettes are selected to meet minimum contrast ratios in both light and dark modes. For details, see the [Colors & Themes](/docs/foundation/colors) documentation.
::

</steps>

## Testing & Verification

We perform [automated accessibility tests](https://playwright.dev/docs/accessibility-testing) on all components across every variation and interactive state.
