## 2. Use of Our Website

### 2.1. Purposes and Legal Bases

When you use our website, the browser on your device automatically collects the following information without any action on your part

- the IP address of the device,
- the date and time of access,
- the name and URL of the file accessed,
- the amount of data transferred,
- the website/application from which the access originated (referrer URL),
- the browser you are using and, if applicable, the operating system of your device, as well as
- the name of your Internet service provider

are sent to our website’s server and temporarily stored in a **log file** for the following purposes:

- to ensure a smooth connection,
- to ensure a convenient user experience on our website/application, and
- to evaluate system security and stability.

The legal basis for this data processing is Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR. The legitimate interest arises from the protection of our systems and the prevention of abusive or fraudulent behavior whenever a user accesses this website.

### 2.2. Recipients

Under certain circumstances, it may be necessary for us to transfer your personal data to other recipients.

The website is operated on our behalf on servers maintained by IT service providers. Your personal data may be made available to these service providers for support and maintenance purposes.

### 2.3. Retention Period/Criteria for Retention

Log files are stored for 30 days.

## 3. Data Subject Rights

If data processing is based on consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) or Art. 9(2)(a) of the GDPR, you may revoke your consent at any time with future effect, without affecting the lawfulness of the processing that has taken place up to that point.

You have the right, pursuant to Article 15(1) of the GDPR, to receive, upon request and free of charge, information about the personal data stored about you.

In addition, provided the legal requirements are met, you have the right to rectification (Article 16 of the GDPR), erasure (Article 17 of the GDPR), and restriction of processing (Article 18 of the GDPR) of your personal data.

If you yourself have provided the processed data, you have the right to data portability under Article 20 of the GDPR.
